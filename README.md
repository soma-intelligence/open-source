## Health

### [Open Health Sync](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/open-health-sync)
One API for every wearable. Apple Health, Health Connect, Garmin, Oura, WHOOP, Fitbit, Withings, Dexcom and more, served as REST, FHIR R4, webhooks and an MCP server. One Go binary plus Postgres. Runs on a Raspberry Pi.

`Go` `Postgres` `Self-hosted` `AGPL-3.0` · [openhealthsync.com](https://openhealthsync.com)

### [Prism](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/prism)
Sleep, recovery, strain, stress and energy scores from whatever your watch, ring or iPhone records. Computed on the device. Free, no account.

`Swift` `iPhone` `iPad` `Mac` `Vision Pro` `AGPL-3.0` · [somalabs.me/prism](https://somalabs.me/prism)

## Privacy

### [OnePass](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/onepass)
A post-quantum password manager. Passkeys, TOTP, AutoFill, a browser extension, an SSH agent and an MCP server. End to end encrypted sync through your own iCloud.

`Rust` `SwiftUI` `iPhone` `Mac` `Watch` `Vision Pro` `AGPL-3.0` · [somalabs.me/onepass](https://somalabs.me/onepass)

### [Soma Relay](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/soma-relay)
A messenger that keeps working when the network doesn't. Post-quantum encryption over the Internet, a Bluetooth and Wi-Fi mesh, or SMS. No account, no phone number.

`Rust` `Swift` `iOS` `Mesh` `AGPL-3.0`

## Tools

### [Soma Flight](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/Soma_Flight)
Live status, gates, delays and Live Activities. A log of every flight with stats, CO2e and your Schengen 90/180 count. Self-hostable backend.

`SwiftUI` `Rust` `Supabase` `iPhone` `Mac` `Watch` `AGPL-3.0`

### [ViaForm](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/ViaForm)
Parametric CAD and PCB design in one app, with the board linked to the 3D model. KiCad export. Native on Mac, iPad and iPhone, Electron on Windows and Linux.

`Rust` `SwiftUI` `Electron` `GPL-3.0`

## Shared principles

- **Your data stays yours.** On your device, your iCloud or your server.
- **Copyleft.** Everything is AGPL or GPL. Audit it, fork it, self-host it.
- **Native first.** Rust cores, SwiftUI apps, one binary where it fits.
